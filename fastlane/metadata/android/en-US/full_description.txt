Lockout is a clean, open-source focus tool designed to help you regain control over your screen time by actively blocking distracting apps.

Features:

* App Blocker: Choose which apps distract you and prevent them from opening during focus sessions.
* Flexible Sessions: Choose timed countdowns or open-ended flow sessions.
* Strict Mode: Prevent canceling sessions early unless you use limited emergency skips.
* Scheduled Routines: Automate your focus periods for work, study, or bedtime.
* Habit Tracking: Maintain daily focus streaks as you build discipline over time.
* Private & Open Source: No accounts required, zero trackers, zero telemetry, and all data stays strictly on your device.
